FAQ

Good to know.

How does Arc know when a trip starts?

With Location set to Always and Motion & Fitness on, Arc notices when you start moving and records the trip on its own, then saves it when you stop. You can also start and stop trips yourself from the Speed tab, the Live Activity, the Watch, or Shortcuts.

How accurate is the speed?

Arc reads your speed from GPS, which is very accurate under open sky. Car speedometers often read a little high, so Arc may show a slightly lower number. Tunnels, tall buildings, and a weak signal make any GPS less accurate, and Arc tells you when the signal is weak.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Your trips are stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account.

What happens to trips older than 7 days without Pro?

They're kept. Arc records and saves every trip whether or not you have Arc Pro. Without it, Trips and Stats show the last 7 days; upgrading opens everything, including trips from before you upgraded.

Can Arc track business mileage?

Yes. Tag a trip as Business once, and turn on Assign Automatically for Business in Settings › Trips › Trip Categories. New trips between the same places are tagged Business on their own. Stats can then show only Business trips for any period, and you can export trips as CSV.

Does Arc use a lot of battery?

GPS uses power whenever it's on. Arc keeps location use light between trips, and Settings lets you choose how automatic recording should be.

Does it work for bike rides and flights?

Yes. Arc records any trip: car, bike, train, or plane. Add your own categories and vehicles to keep them apart.

Is it safe to use while I'm the one at the wheel?

Set Arc up before you leave, and keep your eyes on the road. Arc works on its own, so there's nothing to tap while you're moving. Follow local laws about phone use and mounting.
