Trips aren't starting on their own

  • In the Settings app, go to Arc › Location and choose Always, with Precise Location on.
  • Turn on Motion & Fitness for Arc (Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness).
  • Low Power Mode and Background App Refresh being off can delay automatic trips.

The speed looks wrong

Arc reads speed from GPS. Under open sky it's very accurate, and car speedometers often read a little high. Tunnels, parking structures, tall buildings, and a phone deep in a bag or pocket weaken the signal; Arc shows when the signal is weak.

My trips aren't on my other devices

Trips sync through your own iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices and that iCloud is turned on for Arc (Settings › your name › iCloud). Syncing can take a few minutes.

Exporting or deleting trips

In Arc, go to Settings › Data & Privacy to export every trip as GPX or CSV, or to delete them. Deleting a trip removes it from all your devices.

Chrono
