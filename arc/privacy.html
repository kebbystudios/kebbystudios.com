Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version: Arc doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Your trips are stored on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account, which we can't see.

What Arc uses, and why

  • Location. To show your speed, heading, and elevation, and to record your trips. With Always access, Arc can notice when a trip starts and record it while the app is closed.
  • Motion & Fitness. To tell when you're traveling, so trips start and stop on their own.
  • Audio route. If you link a vehicle to your car's Bluetooth, Arc checks whether your iPhone is connected to it to tag a trip with that vehicle. Arc doesn't listen to or record any audio.

All of this is processed on your devices.

Where your trips are stored

Trips, categories, vehicles, and settings are stored on your iPhone (and Apple Watch). If iCloud is on, they're synced through your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. Kebby Studios has no access to that data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple services

Arc uses Apple Maps to draw maps and to look up place names for the start and end of your trips. To do that, coordinates are sent to Apple, as with any app that uses Apple Maps. Apple doesn't share them with us.

What we collect

Nothing. Arc has no servers of its own and contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code. If you choose to export or share a trip, it goes only where you send it.

If you email us, we'll receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it only to reply.

Children

Arc doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete trips in Arc (Settings › Data & Privacy), or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Arc.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
