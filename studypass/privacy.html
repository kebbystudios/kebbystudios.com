Privacy Policy

StudyPass collects no data.

Effective November 17, 2026

Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your app choices and settings. Apple's Screen Time gives StudyPass only coded tokens for the apps and websites you pick, never their names or your usage history. These tokens and your settings are stored on your iPhone.
  • Your passcode. If you set one, it's stored in the iPhone Keychain as a salted hash, not as the passcode itself.
  • The activity log. StudyPass keeps a short log of what it did (for example "apps locked" or "unlocked after studying"), so problems can be fixed. It stays on your iPhone.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • No servers. StudyPass has no code that sends data over the internet.
  • We don't sell or share data, because we don't have any.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active plan. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

Settings → Help → Diagnostics → Export Diagnostics makes a text file with your StudyPass settings and the activity log. It contains no names of apps or websites. It leaves your iPhone only if you choose to share it, for example by emailing it to us for support. We use a shared file only to answer your question.

Children

A parent can set up StudyPass on a child's iPhone through Family Sharing. StudyPass collects no data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Settings → Help → Diagnostics → Reset All clears your choices and settings. Deleting StudyPass removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
