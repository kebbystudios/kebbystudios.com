Common questions

How do I turn StudyPass off right away?

Settings → Help → Diagnostics → Emergency Stop. It unlocks everything after a 15-second wait. You can also remove StudyPass in iOS Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In StudyPass: Settings → Help → Manage Subscription. Or in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → StudyPass. If you cancel during the free month, you won't be charged.

I bought a plan on another iPhone, or my family has one.

On the plan page, tap Restore Purchases. Plans work with Family Sharing.

I forgot my passcode.

Delete StudyPass and install it again. This also clears your settings.

My apps didn't lock or unlock when expected.

Open StudyPass. If it shows a Repair button, tap it. If that doesn't help, email us a diagnostics file.

The shield's button didn't open my study app.

Check Settings → Study apps to open. Pick the study app you use, and make sure it's also selected in Choose Study Apps, which is what makes its time count.

Can I use StudyPass on my child's iPhone?

Yes. During setup choose My Child, then approve StudyPass with your own Apple Account (the parent's, not your child's). Your child needs to be in your Family Sharing group.
