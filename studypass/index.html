Questions

Which study apps does it work with?

Any app or website you pick. StudyPass recognizes many popular ones, like Anki, Duolingo, Quizlet, Notion, GoodNotes and Khan Academy, and can open them straight from the shield. Time in every study app you pick counts.

Is the lock real?

Yes. StudyPass uses Apple's Screen Time, so your apps are covered by the same shield iOS uses for its own limits. It isn't a reminder you can dismiss.

Can StudyPass see what I do on my phone?

No. Screen Time gives StudyPass coded tokens for the apps you choose, never their names or your usage history. Everything stays on your iPhone, and StudyPass has no servers. Read the privacy policy.

How do I turn it off right away?

Settings → Help → Diagnostics → Emergency Stop. It unlocks everything after a 15-second wait. You can also remove StudyPass in iOS Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access.

Can I set it up on my child's iPhone?

Yes. Choose "My Child" during setup and approve StudyPass with your own Apple Account. Your child needs to be in your Family Sharing group. Plans work with Family Sharing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In StudyPass: Settings → Help → Manage Subscription. Or in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → StudyPass. If you cancel during the free month, you won't be charged.

I bought a plan on another iPhone, or my family has one.

On the plan page, tap Restore Purchases.

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 26.5 or later. Screen Time must be on, and StudyPass will walk you through allowing it.
